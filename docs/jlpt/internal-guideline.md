# Internal N5–N3 guideline and reading support

Implemented from **Language Journey — interne N5–N3-richtlijn en kanji-leessteun — 2026-11-01**. No separate diagnostic test is added. Curriculum words, IDs, lessons, exams, progress and Recall scheduling remain the source of learning activity.

`language-journey-content/internal-guideline.js` owns version `lj-internal-n5-n3-2026-11-01.1`. The cumulative internal anchors are N5 800 lexical words / 100 kanji, N4 1500 / 300 and N3 3500 / 650. These are broad planning estimates, not official lists, passing scores or denominators from the existing external item snapshot. Existing exact word mappings and their provenance remain separate in `jlpt-benchmark.js`; unassigned course words remain visible as a coverage gap. N3 evidence does not require completing N5 first.

Counts deduplicate canonical word IDs; synonyms, forms and directions are not extra words. An explicitly introduced distinct sense keeps its own canonical ID. Dates, weekdays and months each have a cap of **3** in the weighted view. That number is a provisional app policy: the document requires a cap but supplies no numeric cap. Raw lexical counts stay visible. No overall percentage uses these caps.

Evidence is additive in `state.guidelineEvidence`, with its own schema version and original ordinary-task source ID. Seen means introduced, not mastered. Practiced requires a correct explicit word target; applied requires a correct explicit word target in a context task. The app never treats examples, distractors, opened texts, elapsed time or an old aggregate rank as a correct individual answer. Correct previous Recall records can establish practiced evidence, with their original timestamps. Retained evidence does not decay when a card becomes due or a later answer is wrong; due Recall work is reported separately. SRS directions, due dates and intervals are not changed by this guideline.

Kanji evidence requires the existing lexical word, known reading, acknowledged spelling and a correct unassisted target response where that word is actually displayed in kanji. The count deduplicates characters and retains the supporting whole-word readings. Spelling exposure alone is not kanji attainment. Existing deferred spellings, including 日本, stay deferred.

Grammar evidence uses explicit canonical grammar targets. Reading evidence uses the canonical source of a correctly answered source question. Listening requires an explicitly audio-only task; playing visible text does not qualify. Domain task counts are labeled as evidence, not full JLPT coverage. N4/N3 mappings and defensible cross-domain weights are not complete, so the UI supplies no overall or domain mastery percentage. The old external benchmark selector remains available for its source audit, separate from the internal UI.

During an active ordinary lesson question, module, quiz, exam, word practice or Recall card, target words and every answer option/distractor have no kana disclosure or Dictionary entry point. Known non-target context words may retain whole-word kana help. A central runtime guard also rejects stale/native keyboard-activated disclosure controls and direct Dictionary attempts. Audio playback remains available under its existing rules, while protected kana is omitted from audio labels and coach text. Feedback may restore target kana and Dictionary access. Hidden options stay hidden and unfocusable under the existing reveal rules. No new help toggle changes assistance during a test. Outside questions, Dictionary behavior remains unchanged.

Tests cover anchors, caps/raw counts, canonical senses, early N3 learning, seen/practiced/applied distinctions, due-date independence, successful-history retention, kanji word readings, explicit domain evidence, target/options/context boundaries, feedback, stale/keyboard activation, audio text, normal word practice, read-only UI, reload and per-direction SRS preservation. Browser validation is still limited by the existing environment: the connected browser cannot reach the local server and file navigation is blocked.
