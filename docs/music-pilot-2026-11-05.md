# Anime & Muziek — Quintuplets pilot

The optional pilot teaches the catalogued lemmas in blocks of at most three. It reuses the eight existing catalog entries in the inventory and adds the missing words with separate IDs. The words are introduced only when their block is continued. A separate step introduces kanji; the quiz keeps its own score and records only eligible submitted contextual kanji answers through the JLPT adapter. It does not assign a rank, complete a level, or add a second review scheduler. The standard audio registry is consulted; the pilot creates no recordings.
